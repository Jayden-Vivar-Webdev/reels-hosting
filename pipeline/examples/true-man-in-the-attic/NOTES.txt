The man in the attic: Otto Sanhuber lived hidden in Dolly Oesterreich's attics (Milwaukee from 1913, then Los Angeles from 1918). Aug 22 1922 her husband was shot; she was found locked in a closet, claimed a burglar took his diamond watch; Otto had locked her in and gone back up to the attic. He moved with her to the next house's attic. Came out ~8 years later (1930); press called him "the Bat Man"; convicted of manslaughter but freed (statute of limitations); Dolly never convicted (hung jury).
