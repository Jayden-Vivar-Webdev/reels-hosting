The Mary Celeste: left New York 7 Nov 1872 for Genoa with 10 aboard; found adrift near the Azores by the Dei Gratia (Dec 1872), nobody aboard. Seaworthy, food and water aboard, belongings left, 1,700+ barrels of alcohol in the hold; only lifeboat and the captain's navigation instruments gone; one pump disassembled; 3+ ft of water in the hold; last log entry 25 Nov; Gibraltar court found no foul play; the 10 were never seen again.
