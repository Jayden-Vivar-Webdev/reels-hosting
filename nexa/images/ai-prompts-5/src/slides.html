1. Replying to a bad review
An angry reply looks worse than the review. Let AI write the calm first draft.
Here's a 1 star review of my business: [paste review]. Write a short, calm reply. Thank them, own what we got wrong and offer to sort it out by phone. No excuses. Under 60 words.
AI
Hi Mark, thanks for taking the time to tell us. You're right, we should have called when we were running late, and that's on us. We'd like to make it right...
Message
Why it works: the word limit stops it rambling. Read it, make it sound like you, then post.

3. Chasing an unpaid invoice
The email nobody likes writing. Polite and clear gets paid. Vague gets ignored.
Write a polite but firm reminder for invoice [number], [amount], now [X] days overdue. Friendly first line, a clear new due date and my payment link: [link]. Under 80 words.
AI
Hi Tom, hope the new deck is getting plenty of use! Just a quick reminder that invoice 1042 for...
Message
Why it works: "polite but firm" sets the tone, and the link makes paying a one tap job.
