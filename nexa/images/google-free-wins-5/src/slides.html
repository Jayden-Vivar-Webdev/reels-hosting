2. You never claimed your Google Business Profile
It's free. It's the listing that pops up on Maps with your hours, reviews and a call button.

Unclaimed, you can't control any of it.
9:41●●●●
Harlow Electrical
No reviews
Electrician · Penrith NSW
Directions Website

Phone: not listed
Own this business?
Fix: claim it at google.com/business, then add your hours, phone number and real photos.
