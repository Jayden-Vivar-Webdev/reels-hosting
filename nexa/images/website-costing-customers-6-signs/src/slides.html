6 things quietly killing your website
most take 5 minutes to fix. swipe →

1. Nobody can tell what you actually do
If the homepage says "Welcome to our website", a stranger has no clue if you can help them.
yourplumber.example
HOME   ABOUT   CONTACT
Welcome to our website!
We are passionate about excellence.
Fix: say what, who and where. "Blocked drains fixed today, Parramatta."

2. Your phone number isn't tappable
On a phone they have to copy it, switch apps and paste it.

Plenty of people won't bother.
9:41●●●
The Corner Cafe
Open 7 days
Call us to book:
02 5550 4321
thecornercafe.example
Fix: make it a tap to call link, and add a call button that stays on screen.

3. Your contact form wants their life story
yourbusiness.example/contact
Get in touch
Full name *
Company *
Job title *
Budget *
Street address *
Postcode *
How did you hear? *
Fax number *
Fix: ask for name, phone or email and a message. That's it. Get the rest on the call.

4. It takes forever to load
Usually one giant photo straight off a camera.
yourlandscaper.example
Fix: shrink your images, then test your site free at pagespeed.web.dev
@nexaweb_dev

5. No obvious next step
Eight buttons, none of them say "book" or "get a quote".
thecornercafe.example
Learn moreOur storyExploreDiscoverGalleryFind out moreBlogResources
Fix: pick one action, like "Book a table", and put that button near the top and again further down.
@nexaweb_dev

6. It looks like nobody's home
If the latest post is from 2019, people wonder if you're still open.
yourbusiness.example/news
Posted 14 March 2019
Exciting things coming soon!
© 2019 Example Co  ·  Opening hours: TBC
Fix: update the footer year and opening hours, and delete old posts you won't update.
