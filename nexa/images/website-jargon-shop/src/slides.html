01
Domain = your street address
The name people type to find you.
My domains
saltbushbakehouse.com.auActive
Registered toSaltbush Bakehouse
RenewsEvery year
your name, not theirs
Know this: you rent it, usually a year or more at a time. Let it lapse and someone else can buy your address.

02
Hosting = the building
A computer that stores your site and hands it to every visitor.
Hosting plan
Websitesaltbushbakehouse.com.au
Status● Online
BillingMonthly
Know this: it's rent, so it's an ongoing cost. A cheap, crowded building can mean a slow website.

03
DNS = the directions
It tells browsers which building your address belongs to.
DNS records
TypeNamePoints to
A@203.0.113.24
MX@mail server
Know this: move to a new host and this is what gets updated. Get it wrong and your site or email goes missing.
@nexaweb_dev
04
SSL = the lock on the door
It's the https at the start of your address.
With SSL
https://saltbushbakehouse.com.au
Without it
⚠ Not securesaltbushbakehouse.com.au
Know this: it scrambles what people type into your site. Without it, Chrome shows "Not secure" next to your address.

05
CMS = your keys to the shelves
The editor where you change words, prices and photos yourself.

06
SEO = the sign that brings people in
Helping Google understand you, so it shows you to searchers.
sourdough bakery wollongong
saltbushbakehouse.com.au
Sourdough Bakery in Wollongong | Saltbush
Fresh sourdough baked daily from 6am. Order online for pick up.
Know this: it's not magic. Clear page titles, useful pages and a Google Business Profile, done over time.
